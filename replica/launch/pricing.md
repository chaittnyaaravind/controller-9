# Pricing

Controller-9 stays **free**: no ads, no subscription, no paid tier, no account.

Why it matters (replica/feedback.md, 2026-10-26 sample of 454 reviews): ads and paywalls were the most
common complaint — 51 reviews about ads, 56 about price or paywalls, 5 about subscriptions, across 6
sources. Competitors' own pricing pages were not read for this pass, so no price table is given here.

Nothing to set up in Stripe. If a paid option is ever considered, it must not lock a basic control,
show ads during play, or renew silently.
