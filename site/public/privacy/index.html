Privacy

Controller-9 collects no personal data.

Effective 20 October 2026 · applies to the Controller-9 apps for iPhone, Android, Mac, Windows and Linux, and this website.

What we collect

Nothing. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking in the apps or on this website. We do not run servers that receive data from the apps.

How the apps work

The phone app sends controller input (button presses, stick positions, tilt) directly to the computer you pair it with, over your own Wi-Fi, USB or Bluetooth. The connection is end-to-end encrypted and never passes through us or anyone else.

What stays on your devices

  • A device key that identifies your phone to the computers you paired, created on the phone and kept in its secure storage (iPhone Keychain, Android Keystore).
  • The list of computers you paired, your layouts and settings, such as your steering calibration.

Deleting the app deletes all of it. "Forget all computers" in Settings removes the pairings at any time.

Permissions the phone app asks for

  • Camera — only to scan the pairing code shown on your computer. No image is stored or sent.
  • Local network — to find and connect to your computer on your Wi-Fi.
  • Bluetooth — to connect to your computer when Wi-Fi is off or unavailable.
  • Motion sensors — for tilt steering, read only while the controller screen is open and never stored.

Children

Because nothing is collected, there is nothing collected from children either.
