Install Controller-9 on your iPhone.

Free, with your own Apple ID — no App Store needed. Pick one of two ways. AltStore keeps the app refreshed and updated by itself; Sideloadly is the quickest one-off.

Option A · AltStore Recommended · refreshes itself

Add Controller-9 to AltStore Get AltStore

  1. Install AltServer on your computer and AltStore on your iPhone

    Both free from altstore.io (Mac and Windows). AltServer installs AltStore onto the iPhone over a cable, once.

  2. Turn on Developer Mode and trust AltStore

    Settings → Privacy & Security → Developer Mode → on, restart; then Settings → General → VPN & Device Management → your Apple ID → Trust.

  3. Add the Controller-9 source

    Tap Add Controller-9 to AltStore above on your iPhone — or in AltStore go to Sources → + and paste https://controller-9.vercel.app/altstore.json.

  4. Install

    Open Browse, tap Controller-9 → Free. From now on AltStore refreshes it in the background whenever your iPhone and computer share Wi-Fi — which is every time you play — and shows updates when we release them.
