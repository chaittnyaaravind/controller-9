How it works

Three steps. About a minute.

Controller-9 has two parts: the app on your phone, and a small companion on the computer you play on.

01

Install both

The app on your phone, the companion on your computer. Both are free.

02

Pair once

Scan the code on the computer. The link is encrypted end to end and the pairing is remembered.

03

Play

Choose a layout and go. Wi-Fi and Bluetooth can run together, so a dropped link doesn't drop you.

Layouts

The controller you already know.

Five familiar faces, drawn to fit your phone. Buttons are positional: the bottom face button always sends A, whatever it is called. Racing, arcade and custom layouts too.

Feel

Built to be accurate.

Tilt steering

Straight ahead is however you hold it.

Steering is measured from your own grip, not from the phone being upright. Hold it tilted, lean back, recenter any time — the wheel stays where your hands are.

Touch

One press, one control.

Round buttons are touched as circles, the D-pad favours straight directions over diagonals, and a thumb resting in the gap between buttons presses nothing.

Rumble

Games can shake your hands.

Two-motor rumble from the game, rendered on the phone's own haptics — heavy hits pulse, light ones hum.
